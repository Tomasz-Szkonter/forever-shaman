# ⚡ Forever Shaman Wiki

Welcome to the **Forever Shaman Wiki**, a community knowledge bank for playing **Shaman in World of Warcraft: Forever**.

WoW Forever is Blizzard's "Classic+": a permanent, level-60 fork of WoW Classic Era with new races, zones, talents and systems. This wiki explains how Shaman mechanics **actually work**. Each page starts from the Classic baseline, then documents what Forever changed. Every fact is sourced and tagged by confidence.

> **Current data:** beta build `1.60.1.70170` (Oct 1, 2026, level cap 30) · **Launch:** Nov 4, 2026
> **Legend:** ✅ tested by us · 🔵 Blizzard-confirmed · 🟢 client data · 🟡 community · 🔴 unverified ([details](../CONTRIBUTING.md#2-confidence-markers))
