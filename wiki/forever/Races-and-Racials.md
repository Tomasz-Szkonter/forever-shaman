## 👥 Who Can Be a Shaman

- **Classic Era:** Shaman is **Horde-only**: Orc, Tauren and Troll.
- 🔵 **Forever:** **Dwarf Shaman** is one of six new race and class combinations, and the new **Windshaper Skyborne** can be a Shaman too ([Deep Dive][deepdive], [Meet the Skyborne][skyborne]).

| Faction | Race | Status | Conf. |
|---|---|---|---|
| Horde | Orc | Returning | 🔵🟢 |
| Horde | Tauren | Returning | 🔵🟢 |
| Horde | Troll | Returning | 🔵🟢 |
| Horde | **Windshaper Skyborne** | **New race**. Needs the paid **Skyborne Heroic Pack** or higher | 🔵 [Meet the Skyborne][skyborne] |
| Alliance | **Dwarf** | **New combination**. The **only Alliance Shaman** | 🔵 [Deep Dive][deepdive] |

### Who cannot be a Shaman

- 🟢 **Human, Night Elf, Gnome, Undead and High Order Skyborne** (the Alliance Skyborne) **cannot** be Shamans ([ForeverChanges race and class matrix][fc-racials], [Warcraft Tavern][wt-combos]).
- 🟢 **There is no Draenei** in Forever ([ForeverChanges][fc-racials], [Warcraft Tavern Shaman guide][wt]).
- 🔵 The six new combinations are **Gnome Priest, Human Hunter, Dwarf Shaman, Orc Mage, Troll Warlock and Undead Paladin** ([Deep Dive][deepdive]).
- 🔴 A fetch of warcraft.wiki.gg listed **Undead** as Shaman-capable, along with a different "six new combos" list. That contradicts the official list and the client matrix, so treat it as an error on that page ([warcraft.wiki.gg][wiki-forever]).

### Skyborne purchase requirement

- 🔵 "Access to Skyborne Race or the Zephras Isle starting experience questline requires purchase of Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher edition." No later free unlock has been announced ([Warcraft Tavern editions][wt-editions], quoting Blizzard's terms).
- 🟡 The Heroic Pack costs **$29.99** ([Warcraft Tavern editions][wt-editions]).
- 🔵 Windshaper (Horde) Skyborne can be **Druid, Hunter, Rogue, Shaman or Warrior**. High Order (Alliance) Skyborne can be Druid, Hunter, Mage, Rogue or Warrior ([Meet the Skyborne][skyborne]).

## 🔄 Racial Redesign

These rules apply to every race in Forever:

- 🔵 "Each race now has **two active abilities and two passive abilities**, tuned to provide a similar amount of offensive power while preserving signature utility." ([Deep Dive][deepdive])
- 🟡🟢 **All resistance racials are gone.** Racial weapon-skill bonuses became **+1% crit** with that weapon type ([ForeverChanges][fc-racials], [Icy Veins snippet][iv-racials]).
- 🔵 Racials were tuned during the beta. Examples outside Shaman races: Gnome Eureka! became a 10% resource discount and then stopped affecting periodic effects. Tauren **Cultivation** gained level requirements ([dev notes][blz]).

## ⚔️ Racials by Race

Sources for this section: 🟢 [ForeverChanges racials][fc-racials] (build 1.60.1.70170) and 🟡 [Method.gg][method-racials]. Skyborne text is 🔵 from [Meet the Skyborne][skyborne]. "Changed" and "new" are relative to Classic Era. Cooldowns and exact durations not listed here were not in the sources (see [To Verify](#-to-verify)).

### Orc

| Type | Racial | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Active | **Blood Fury** (changed) | +10% Attack Power **and Spell Power** for 15 s |
| Active | **Shatter Curse** (new) | Removes curses and banes; −15% magic damage taken for 8 s |
| Passive | **Axe Specialization** | +1% crit with one- and two-handed axes |
| Passive | **Hardiness** | Stun duration −20% |

**For Shaman:**
- Blood Fury now boosts **spell power** as well as attack power, so it helps Elemental and Enhancement alike.
- Shatter Curse fills a gap: the Shaman spellbook has Cure Poison and Cure Disease but **no curse removal** 🟢 ([ForeverChanges spellbook][fc]).
- Axe Specialization helps an Enhancement Shaman using an axe. Hardiness is a PvP defensive.
- 🟡 Warcraft Tavern calls Orc "**the best choice for Horde Shamans**" ([WT][wt]). PvP guides also point to Orc racials ([Icy Veins][iv], [WT][wt]).

### Tauren

| Type | Racial | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Active | **War Stomp** | 2 s area stun, 8 yd, up to 5 targets |
| Active | **Cultivation** (changed) | Duplicate nearby herbs without Herbalism. Level-gated: herb skill ÷ 5 |
| Passive | **Plainsrunning** (new) | +1% movement speed per 5 s of moving, up to 30% |
| Passive | **Endurance** | +5% Health **and +1% hit** |

**For Shaman:**
- The **+1% hit** from Endurance helps every spec. With unified hit, it should count for both spells and melee (inference from the [unified hit rule](Forever-System-Changes.md#unified-hit-and-crit)).
- War Stomp is an area interrupt and escape tool. Plainsrunning helps travel before Ghost Wolf (level 20).

### Troll

| Type | Racial | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Active | **Berserking** (changed) | **Flat +10% cast and attack speed** for 10 s |
| Active | **Rapid Regeneration** (new) | Heals 50% of max health over 6 s |
| Passive | **Beast Slaying** | +5% damage vs Beasts |
| Passive | **Regeneration** | +10% health regeneration; 10% continues in combat |

**For Shaman:**
- Berserking is **no longer scaled by missing health**. It is a flat 10% haste window for casting **and** melee, so it helps every spec.
- Rapid Regeneration is a self-heal cooldown. Beast Slaying helps while leveling against beasts.

### Windshaper Skyborne

| Type | Racial | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Active | **Walk on Air** | Glide for 10 s |
| Active | **Skysight** | +10% run speed (30 s base; 15 min near a "convergence") |
| Passive | **Wind Blessed** | +1% melee, ranged **and spell haste** |
| Passive | **Elemental Insight** | +5% damage to Elementals |

**For Shaman:**
- Wind Blessed gives haste, a rare stat in Forever. 🟡 Warcraft Tavern had not seen haste on any gear yet ([WT stats][wt-stats]).
- Elemental Insight applies only against Elementals. 🟢 Two Shaman Spiritcaller 4-piece bonuses also target Elementals (+36 AP and +21 spell damage vs Elementals) ([wow.gg][wowgg-sets]).
- Walk on Air and Skysight are movement utilities.
- 🔵 Lore: Highborne exiles who made a pact with wind spirits at Skywall. Windshapers follow the elemental path and look to the Earthen Ring ([Meet the Skyborne][skyborne]).

### Dwarf

| Type | Racial | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Active | **Stoneform** | Removes bleeds, poisons and diseases; −10% physical damage taken for 8 s |
| Active | **Find Treasure** | Treasure finding (no further detail in sources) |
| Passive | **Mace Specialization** (new) | +1% crit with maces |
| Passive | **Big Game Hunter** (new) | +5% damage vs Beasts |

**For Shaman:**
- Dwarf is the **only Alliance Shaman**.
- Mace Specialization helps an Enhancement Shaman using a mace.
- Stoneform is a defensive cleanse, mostly useful in PvP and dungeons.
- 🟡 The Dwarf Priest racial changes moved Fear Ward to baseline for all Priests ([Warcraft Tavern][wt-priest]). This does not affect Shaman but shows how far the Dwarf kit changed.

## 🏁 Starting Out and Trainers

### Skyborne

- 🔵 Skyborne start on **Zephras Isle** (levels 1–12) ([Meet the Skyborne][skyborne]).
- 🔵 Oct 1 notes: "**Shenthul in Orgrimmar will now train Windshaper Skyborne.**" The note does not say which class this concerns ([dev notes][blz]). See [To Verify](#-to-verify).
- 🟡 The Skyborne earth totem quest (*Call of the Earth*): steal a ring for a cultist, drink a sapta, fight an earth elemental at a stone, then receive a shard to craft the totem. Players called it thematically weak ([forum thread][f-skyquest]).

### Dwarf

- 🟡 Dwarves have a **new totem quest chain**. *Call of Earth* (level 4) starts with **Teo Hammerstorm in Anvilmar**. *Call of Fire* (10) involves Dun Morogh and Loch Modan, and *Call of Water* (20) sends you through Wetlands, Redridge and Ashenvale (about 2 hours of travel). *Call of Air* (30) is not documented yet ([Warcraft Tavern Dwarf totem quests][wt-dwarf]).
- Full quest steps: [Class Quests](../reference/Class-Quests.md)

### All races

- 🟡 Totem unlock levels: **Earth 4, Fire 10, Water 20, Air 30** ([WT][wt]).
- 🟡 Two-handed axes and maces are **baseline** for Shaman in Forever (no talent needed) ([Zockify][zk], [WT][wt]).

## 🧭 Choosing a Race

The sources give only a little race advice so far:
- 🟡 **Horde:** Warcraft Tavern recommends **Orc** as "the best choice for Horde Shamans" ([WT][wt]).
- 🟡 **PvP:** guides point to **Orc** racials ([Icy Veins][iv], [WT][wt]).
- **Alliance:** Dwarf is the only option.
- No source has published per-spec race rankings for Elemental, Enhancement or Restoration. The beta is capped at level 30, so racial value at level 60 is untested.
