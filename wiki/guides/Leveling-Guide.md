# 🧗 Leveling Guide

> ⚠️ **Status: provisional.** The beta is capped at **level 30** (20 until Oct 1). Everything above level 30 in this guide is **client data only** and has never been played live. No source has published a full 1–60 Forever leveling route or a "best leveling spec".

## 📅 Key Unlock Levels

🟢 **Rank counts and learn levels are identical to Classic** for every spell checked, except Mana Tide Totem R1 ~~40~~ → **25** (the talent moved up) ([WCLBox](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/en/fashu/shaman), [ForeverChanges](https://foreverchanges.pro/spellbook/shaman)). New trainer spells: Call of the Elements (20), Totemic Recall (20), Totemic Projection (22), Call of the Ancestors (30), Call of the Spirits (40). Fire Nova Totem became the spell **Fire Nova** at the same levels (12/22/32/42/52).

## 🗿 Totem Quests

🟡 Totem items and quests still exist: **Earth at 4, Fire at 10, Water at 20, Air at 30** ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/), [Warcraft Tavern Dwarf quests](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/dwarf-shaman-totem-quests/)). Full walkthroughs per race: [Class Quests](../reference/Class-Quests.md).

- **Dwarf (new Alliance chain)** 🟡 ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/dwarf-shaman-totem-quests/)):
  - *Call of Earth* (4), Teo Hammerstorm in Anvilmar: rewards the **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem.
  - *Call of Fire* (10), Dun Morogh and Loch Modan: rewards the **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem. Mobs go up to level 14.
  - *Call of Water* (20), Loch Modan and Wetlands, with samples from Wetlands, Redridge and Ashenvale: rewards the **Water Totem** and Healing Stream Totem. Takes about **2 hours** of travel.
  - *Call of Air* (30): not documented yet.
- **Windshaper Skyborne** *Call of the Earth*: steal a ring for a cultist, drink a sapta, fight an earth elemental, then craft the totem from a shard. Players called it thematically weak 🟡 ([forum thread](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/call-of-the-earth-disappointing-skyborne-quest/2365329)).
- Orc, Tauren and Troll quests: see [Class Quests](../reference/Class-Quests.md).
- In Classic, every totem tooltip requires its element's tool ("Tools: Air Totem" and so on) ([Allakhazam Shaman FAQ](https://wow.allakhazam.com/wiki/Comprehensive_Shaman_FAQ_%28WoW%29)). Analysis: do each quest as soon as it unlocks so the new totems are usable. No source confirms whether Forever still uses four separate totem items in your bags.

## 🐺 Ghost Wolf and Getting Around

- 🟢 **[Ghost Wolf](../reference/Spells-and-Ranks.md#ghost-wolf)** at level 20: 100 mana, **3 s cast**, +40% run speed, **outdoors only** ([WCLBox](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/en/fashu/shaman/shaman-ghost-wolf)). 🔴 Icy Veins and Conquest Capped say 2 s, probably the BlizzCon value.
- 🟢 **[Improved Ghost Wolf](../talents/Enhancement-Talents.md#improved-ghost-wolf)** (Enhancement row 2) cuts the cast by 1 / 3 s, so **2/2 is instant**, and lets you use Ghost Wolf **indoors** ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)).
- 🔵 Ghost Wolf was made more visible in bright areas on Oct 1 ([dev notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)); 🟡 players now say it is too bright ([thread 2369180](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2369180)).
- 🟢 **Astral Recall** (30) is unchanged.
- 🔵 **Mounts:** riding follows the TBC model — the cost moves into training, and you get a mount when you learn riding ([Blizzard Deep Dive recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap)). The riding level and price are not known.

## 🌳 Leveling Talents

- 🟡 **Improved Ghost Wolf first:** at level 20, put points into Improved Ghost Wolf for mobility, whatever your spec ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/)). Analysis: it sits in Enhancement row 2, so you need 5 Enhancement points first.
- 🟡 **Dips that include it:** Restoration players can take 12 Enhancement points for instant indoor Ghost Wolf + 5% crit + 10% Intellect; Elemental players can take 11 points for Thundering Strikes and Ancestral Knowledge plus 1 point of Improved Ghost Wolf ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/), [Icy Veins overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/shaman-class-overview)).
- **Which spec?** No source names a best leveling spec. Data points only:
  - 🟡 Popularity: 58% of complete Shaman builds on wowforevertalent.com are Enhancement, 24% Elemental, 18% Restoration ([calculator](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/)).
  - 🟡 Sims: mythicsim's reference Enhancement (622 DPS) ranks well above its Elemental (398 DPS); "values still being checked" ([mythicsim](https://mythicsim.com/wow-forever/shaman)).
- **Level-30 builds** from Warcraft Tavern and Icy Veins are in each spec guide: [Elemental](Elemental-Guide.md), [Enhancement](Enhancement-Guide.md), [Restoration](Restoration-Guide.md). ⚠️ Warcraft Tavern's Elemental screenshot uses the old tree and is not legal now.
- 🔵 **Respec** at a class trainer in a major city. In the beta it costs 1 silver, rising after the first reset and dropping back after an hour ([dev notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696), [Blizzard Class Deep Dive](https://worldofwarcraft.blizzard.com/en-gb/news/24301515)). See [Talent Overview](../talents/Talent-Overview.md#respec-and-dual-spec).

## 🎒 Weapons and Gear While Leveling

- 🟡 **Caster staves were nerfed:** Staff of Westfall shows 20.50 DPS on Wowhead but **15.2 DPS in game**, and Crescent Staff is no longer better than a one-hander for Shaman leveling ([thread 2359184](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2359184), [thread 2365746](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2365746)). **Don't trust Wowhead weapon DPS for Forever** until its item data updates.
- 🔵 Caster weapons now carry spell power from around level 10 ([Blizzard Deep Dive recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap)).
- 🟡 **Two-handed axes and maces are baseline** (the talent is gone) ([Zockify](https://www.zockify.com/forever/shaman/)). Shamans use axes, maces, staves, fist weapons, daggers and shields, plus relic-slot totems ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/)).
- 🟢 **Relics** usable early: *Firestorm Totem* (totem mana −5%) and *Windcarved Effigy* (Lightning Bolt −5 mana) at any level; *Polished Driftwood Icon* (8% regen while casting) at 20 ([WoWHandbook relics](https://wowhandbook.com/gear/relics/); [Items and Set Bonuses](../reference/Items-and-Set-Bonuses.md)).
- 🟡 **Mail at 40** ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/)).

## 💡 Leveling Tips from the Beta

- 🔵 **Train every new rank.** Since Oct 1, spells cast at ranks far below your level get less spell power and a reduced chance to proc talents ([dev notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)). 🔵 Lightning Bolt R3 and R4 were buffed on Sept 24 so each rank is always an upgrade.
- 🔵 **Solo pace:** Blizzard says solo mobs take about 10–15 seconds ([Blizzard Deep Dive recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap)).
- 🔵 **Dungeon quests** give 50% less bonus XP since Sept 24 ([dev notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)).
- 🟡 **Threat in low-level dungeons:** Rockbiter and Earth Shock have high threat modifiers, and Fire Nova is "a massive threat generator even against single target". There is no single-target taunt ([thread 2365062](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2365062), [thread 2366147](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2366147)). See [Threat](../mechanics/Threat.md).
- 🟡 **Call of the Elements** (3.0 s cast) only saves about 1 s when you need all 4 totems; drop 3 or fewer by hand ([thread 2369645](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2369645)).
- 🔵 **Totemic Recall** (20) returns 25% of each recalled totem's mana (fixed Oct 1) ([dev notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)). 🟡 Below level 20 the only way to dismiss a totem is right-clicking the totem frame; the old `/click TotemFrameTotemN RightButton` macro does nothing in Forever ([thread 2364945](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2364945)). See [Macros](../reference/Macros.md).
- 🟢 **Totem uptime:** most buff totems now last ~~2 min~~ → **5 min** with ~~20~~ → **30 yd** range ([ForeverChanges](https://foreverchanges.pro/spellbook/shaman)). See [Totem Mechanics](../mechanics/Totem-Mechanics.md).
- 🔵 **XP food:** professions now include food that gives an XP bonus ([Blizzard Deep Dive recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap)).

## 📜 Legacy System Notes

- 🔵 The **Legacy System** is account-wide horizontal progression: 65 challenges, usually starting around character level 25, and 16 points per character at launch, spent in Professions, Adventure and Resourcefulness trees (examples: Reagent Economy, Well Rested). Changing Legacy talents requires a class trainer. Hardcore does not share Legacy progress ([Blizzard Legacy System](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24307383/)).
- 🟡 The **Talented** perk grants up to **5 extra talent points**, so a level-30 character can have 26 ([Icy Veins Elemental](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/elemental-shaman-ranged-dps-pve-guide)). See [Talent Overview](../talents/Talent-Overview.md#legacy-talented-perk).
