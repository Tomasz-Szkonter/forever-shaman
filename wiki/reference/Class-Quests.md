# 🪨 Class Quests

Shamans still earn their elemental totems through class quests in WoW Forever. This page lists the unlock levels, the new **Dwarf** quest chain (the first Alliance Shamans), the **Skyborne** version, and what we do not know yet about the Horde chains.

## 🔓 Unlock Levels

### Totem Quest Levels

🟡 **Totem items and their quests still exist.** Each element unlocks at a fixed level ([Warcraft Tavern Shaman guide][wt-shaman], [Warcraft Tavern Dwarf quests][wt-dwarf]):

| Element | Quest level | First totem it unlocks (Dwarf chain) |
|---|---|---|
| Earth | **4** | [Stoneskin Totem](Totems.md#stoneskin-totem) |
| Fire | **10** | [Searing Totem](Totems.md#searing-totem) |
| Water | **20** | [Healing Stream Totem](Totems.md#healing-stream-totem) |
| Air | **30** | — (not documented yet) |

- These levels match the first totem of each element on the trainer list: Stoneskin at 4, Searing at 10, Healing Stream at 20, and Grounding / Nature Resistance at 30 🟢 ([Totems](Totems.md)).
- The beta reached level 30 on Oct 1, so the Air quest has only just become reachable 🔵 ([dev notes][blz-notes]).
- New at level 20: **Totemic Recall**, **Call of the Elements** and the Totem Bar ([Spells and Ranks](Spells-and-Ranks.md#call-of-the-elements)). 🟡 Below level 20, the only way to dismiss a totem is to right-click it on the totem frame ([forum thread 2364945][f-2364945]).

## 🗺 Quest Chains by Race

### Dwarf Totem Quest Chain

🟡 Dwarf is a **new Shaman race** and the only Alliance Shaman. Its chain was documented by [Warcraft Tavern][wt-dwarf]:

| Quest | Level | Givers / area | Steps | Reward |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| *Call of Earth* | 4 | Teo Hammerstorm, Anvilmar | Collect 2 Iceclaw Bear Pendants, drink the Earth Sapta at the Spirit Stone, kill the Minor Manifestation of Earth | **Earth Totem** and Stoneskin Totem |
| *Call of Fire* | 10 | Bruegs Kindleborn (Dun Morogh) and Braldir Ashmantle (Loch Modan) | Collect Fire Tar and a Reagent Pouch, drink the Fire Sapta, kill the Minor Manifestation of Fire. Mobs go up to level 14 | **Fire Totem** and Searing Totem |
| *Call of Water* | 20 | Norric Lochthane (Loch Modan) and Hervdana Saegrund (Wetlands) | Collect water samples in Wetlands, Redridge and Ashenvale, drink the Water Sapta at the Forgotten Shrine, kill the Corrupt Minor Manifestation of Water. About **2 hours of travel** | **Water Totem** and Healing Stream Totem |
| *Call of Air* | 30 | — | Not documented yet | — |

- Dwarf racials for Shamans are on [Races and Racials](../forever/Races-and-Racials.md).

### Skyborne Totem Quests

🟡 **Windshaper Skyborne** (the Horde Skyborne; requires the Skyborne Heroic Pack or higher) can be Shamans 🔵 ([Blizzard: Meet the Skyborne][blz-skyborne]). Their *Call of the Earth* quest, as players describe it ([forum thread "Call of the Earth: disappointing Skyborne quest"][f-skyborne]):

1. Steal a ring for a cultist.
2. Drink a sapta.
3. Fight an earth elemental at a stone.
4. Receive a shard to **craft** the totem.

- Players called it thematically weak; no Blizzard reply was seen.
- Skyborne start on Zephras Isle (levels 1–12) 🔵 ([Blizzard][blz-skyborne]). Skyborne Shaman totem models were revealed by Wowhead ([news 382948][wh-382948]; article body not read).
- The Fire, Water and Air quests for Skyborne are not documented.

### Orc, Tauren and Troll

🔴 **Not documented for Forever.** Our sources only say that totem quests "still exist" with the unlock levels above ([Warcraft Tavern][wt-shaman]). The Classic Horde quest chains are **presumably** still in the game, but no source confirms their givers, steps or rewards in Forever. We will not copy Classic quest details here until someone checks them in the beta.

### Not a Shaman Quest

🔵 The Oct 1 notes add a quest, "Power Over Poison", that teaches **Cure Poison** to **High Order Skyborne** ([dev notes][blz-notes]). High Order is the Alliance Skyborne race, which cannot be a Shaman ([Races and Racials](../forever/Races-and-Racials.md)), so this is not the Shaman spell of the same name.

## 🎒 Totem Items

### Four Totems or One Tool

🔴 **Unknown.** The Dwarf quests reward separate **Earth, Fire and Water Totem** items 🟡 ([Warcraft Tavern][wt-dwarf]), which suggests the Classic model of one item per element is still in place. But no source states whether Forever later merges them into a single tool, or whether they still take bag slots.

- The relic-slot "totems" (Firestorm Totem, Tidal Totem, and so on) are **equipment**, not the quest totems. See [Items and Set Bonuses](Items-and-Set-Bonuses.md#totem-relics).
- Reincarnation still consumes an **Ankh** 🟢 ([Spells and Ranks](Spells-and-Ranks.md#reincarnation)).
