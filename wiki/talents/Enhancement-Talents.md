# 🪓 Enhancement Talents

This page covers every talent in the Forever **Enhancement** tree, rank by rank, against Classic Era. Stormstrike moved up to the 16-point row, two-handed axes and maces became baseline, and the tree now ends in **Rage of the Farseer**. For tree rules and respecs, see the [Talent Overview](Talent-Overview.md). For how to play the spec, see the [Enhancement Guide](../guides/Enhancement-Guide.md).

> **Build:** `1.60.1.70170` · **Legend:** ✅ tested here · 🔵 Blizzard · 🟢 client data · 🟡 community · 🔴 unverified — see [Contributing](../../CONTRIBUTING.md#2-confidence-markers)
> **Classic baseline:** Classic Era 1.15.9.69722, compared rank by rank by [wowforevertalents.com](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/).
> **Beta cap is level 30** (21 points, or up to 26 with the Legacy *Talented* perk). Maelstrom Weapon (row 6) can get at most 1 point at that cap, and **Rage of the Farseer has never been available live in the beta**.

## 🌳 Talents

Effect text comes from client tooltips as read by [wowforevertalent.com](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/reference/) (wft) and [wowforevertalents.com](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/) (wfts) 🟢. Where they disagree, wfts wins and the other value is noted.

### Earth's Grasp

**Row 1, Col 1 · 2 ranks · Requires: — · Status: `MOVED` (Elemental (2,1) → Enhancement (1,1))**

- 🟢 **Forever:** Stoneclaw Totem health `+25 / 50%`; Earthbind Totem radius `+10 / 20%` ([wft](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/reference/)).
- **vs Classic:** identical text; it was an Elemental talent in Classic.
- See [Stoneclaw Totem](../reference/Totems.md#stoneclaw-totem) and [Earthbind Totem](../reference/Totems.md#earthbind-totem).

### Thundering Strikes

**Row 1, Col 2 · 5 ranks · Requires: — · Status: `CHANGED` + `MOVED` (row 2 → row 1)**

- 🟢 **Forever:** critical strike chance with **all spells and attacks** `+1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5%` ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)).
- **vs Classic:** ~~weapon attacks only~~ (1–5%) ([WH Classic 16302](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=16302)) → **all spells and attacks**.
- 🔵 Context: Forever unifies spell, melee and ranged crit into one value ([Blizzard Deep Dive recap](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24303313/world-of-warcraft-forever-deep-dive-panel-recap)). Analysis: that is why one talent now covers both.
- Because it boosts spells too, it shows up in Elemental and Restoration dips: Warcraft Tavern and Icy Veins suggest an 11-point Enhancement dip for Elemental and a 12-point dip for Restoration 🟡 ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/), [Icy Veins overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/shaman-class-overview)).
- Most-picked Shaman talent on wowforevertalent.com (90% of builds; popularity data) 🟡 ([calculator stats](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/)).

### Ancestral Knowledge

**Row 1, Col 3 · 5 ranks · Requires: — · Status: `CHANGED`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** **Intellect** `+2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10%` ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)).
- **vs Classic:** ~~maximum Mana +1 / 2 / 3 / 4 / 5%~~ ([WH Classic 17487](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=17487)) → **Intellect +2–10%**.
- Icy Veins' overview says it went "from 1% to 2% Intellect"; that is wrong, since Classic gave max mana, not Intellect ([Icy Veins overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/shaman-class-overview)).
- Analysis: with [Mental Dexterity](#mental-dexterity) and [Mental Quickness](#mental-quickness) turning Intellect into attack power and spell power, this talent feeds Enhancement damage as well as mana. Picked in 75% of builds (popularity data) 🟡 ([calculator stats](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/)).

### Guardian Totems

**Row 2, Col 1 · 2 ranks · Requires: — · Status: `UNCHANGED`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** Stoneskin and Windwall Totem reduction `+10 / 20%`; Grounding Totem cooldown `−1 / 2 sec` ([wft](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/reference/)).
- **vs Classic:** identical.
- 🟡 A forum post works it out as "30 physical damage reduction, ×1.2 with Guardian Totems = 36" for max-rank Stoneskin ([thread 2367626](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2367626)).

### Mental Dexterity

**Row 2, Col 2 · 3 ranks · Requires: — (unlocks Flurry) · Status: `NEW`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** Attack Power increased by `33 / 67 / 100%` of your Intellect ([wft](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/reference/), [wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)).
- **vs Classic:** did not exist.
- Beta rounding: 66/99% → 67/100% ([wfts beta](https://wowforevertalents.com/beta/)).
- It is the gateway to [Flurry](#flurry), so every Enhancement build needs 3/3. See [Stat Mechanics](../mechanics/Stat-Mechanics.md).

### Improved Ghost Wolf

**Row 2, Col 3 · 2 ranks · Requires: — · Status: `CHANGED`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** Ghost Wolf cast time `−1 / 3 sec`, and Ghost Wolf becomes **usable indoors** ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)).
- **vs Classic:** ~~−1 / 2 sec, outdoors only~~ → **−1 / 3 sec, indoors allowed**.
- 🟢 Base Ghost Wolf is a **3 sec** cast in the client, so **2/2 makes it instant** ([wfts abilities](https://wowforevertalents.com/abilities/shaman/), [WCLBox](https://wowforever.wclbox.com/en/fashu/shaman/shaman-ghost-wolf)). 🔴 Icy Veins and Conquest Capped give the base as 2 sec, which is probably the stale BlizzCon value ([Icy Veins overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/shaman-class-overview), [Conquest Capped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-shaman-talents/)). Beta history: rank 2 was −2 sec before it became −3 sec ([wfts beta](https://wowforevertalents.com/beta/)).
- 🟡 Warcraft Tavern says to put points into it **first** at level 20 for mobility, whatever your spec ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/)). Picked in 71% of builds (popularity) 🟡 ([calculator stats](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/)).
- See [Ghost Wolf](../reference/Spells-and-Ranks.md#ghost-wolf) and the [Leveling Guide](../guides/Leveling-Guide.md).

### Flurry

**Row 4, Col 2 · 5 ranks · Requires: Mental Dexterity 3/3 · Status: `CHANGED` (weaker per rank)**

- 🟢 **Forever:** after a **melee** critical strike, attack speed `+5 / 10 / 15 / 20 / 25%` for your next 3 swings ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)).
- **vs Classic:** ~~+10 / 15 / 20 / 25 / 30% after any critical strike~~ ([WH Classic 16284](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=16284)) → **5–25%, melee crits only**.
- Beta history: it once lasted for the next **3 × rank** swings (up to 15); now it is the next **3** swings ([wfts beta](https://wowforevertalents.com/beta/)).
- Classic note: the Flurry buff has a 0.5 s lockout on charge use, so two swings within 500 ms spend only one charge ([Classic Era DB2](https://wago.tools/db2/SpellAuraOptions?product=wow_classic_era)). Not checked in Forever.
- See [Enhancement Mechanics](../mechanics/Enhancement-Mechanics.md#flurry).

### Stormstrike

**Row 4, Col 3 · 1 rank · Requires: — · Status: `CHANGED` + `MOVED` (row 7 → row 4, now a 16-point active)**

- 🟢 **Forever:** active. **125 mana, melee range, instant, 8 sec cooldown.** Strikes for normal weapon damage; the target then takes **+20% damage from your next Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning or Earth Shock** within 12 sec ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/), [ClassicWoWForever](https://classicwowforever.com/class-changes/shaman/)).
- **vs Classic:** ~~extra attack; next 2 Nature damage sources on the target +20% for 12 sec; 20 sec cooldown; 31-point capstone~~ ([WH Classic 17364](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=17364)) → **next LB, CL or Earth Shock +20%; 8 sec cooldown; 16-point talent**. Classic's mana cost is disputed (15% vs 21% of base mana).
- **Trainer ranks:** none; 1 rank only.
- Beta history: during early beta it gave "+20% Nature damage taken for 12 sec"; it now buffs only the next LB/CL/ES ([wfts beta](https://wowforevertalents.com/beta/)). 🟡 Power Up Gaming describes it as raising "the Shaman's own Nature damage" rather than the target's damage taken ([Power Up Gaming](https://powerupgaming.co.uk/2026/09/14/new-wow-forever-talents-changes-vs-classic-guide/)); the current tooltip still reads as a mark on the target.
- Gear: *Spiritcaller's Rage* 5-piece reduces its cooldown by 0.5 sec, and the *Totem of the Undergrove* relic lets Shocks against Undead cut it by 0.5 sec 🟢 ([wow.gg](https://wow.gg/guides/wow-forever-tier-set-bonuses), [WoWHandbook relics](https://wowhandbook.com/gear/relics/); [Items and Set Bonuses](../reference/Items-and-Set-Bonuses.md)).
- See [Spells and Ranks](../reference/Spells-and-Ranks.md#stormstrike) and [Enhancement Mechanics](../mechanics/Enhancement-Mechanics.md#stormstrike).

### Spirit Weapons

**Row 5, Col 1 · 1 rank · Requires: — · Status: `RENAMED from Parry`, `CHANGED`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** gives a chance to parry. Threat **−30% without Rockbiter**, **+30% with Rockbiter Weapon** active ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)).
- **vs Classic (Parry):** ~~parry chance only, no threat effect~~ ([WH Classic 16268](https://www.wowhead.com/classic/spell=16268)). A threat-reducing Spirit Weapons did not exist in Classic; it was a TBC talent.
- 🟡 A tester confirms "-30% threat reduction … when not using Rockbiter" ([thread 2365734](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2365734)).
- 🟡 Icy Veins says Enhancement can tank dungeons "in a pinch" with Spirit Weapons and Rockbiter ([Icy Veins overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/shaman-class-overview); [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/)). See [Threat](../mechanics/Threat.md).

### Mental Quickness

**Row 5, Col 2 · 2 ranks · Requires: — (unlocks Rage of the Farseer) · Status: `NEW`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** spell damage and healing increased by up to `15 / 30%` of your Intellect ([wft](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/reference/), [wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)). DB2 spell 30812 has both effects at base 30 ([wago SpellEffect](https://wago.tools/db2/SpellEffect/csv?build=1.60.1.70170)).
- **vs Classic:** did not exist.
- 🔴 Early previews said **100%** of Intellect ([thread 2354160](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2354160)). Client data says 30% at 2/2.
- It is the gateway to [Rage of the Farseer](#rage-of-the-farseer).

### Improved Stormstrike

**Row 5, Col 3 · 2 ranks · Requires: Stormstrike 1/1 · Status: `NEW`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** when you Stormstrike, a `50 / 100%` chance to gain **50% mana regeneration while casting for 15 sec**; and a `50 / 100%` chance for Stormstrike's cooldown to **reset when the target dodges or parries** ([wft](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/reference/), [wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)). Spell ID 1223031.
- **vs Classic:** did not exist.
- Beta history: rank 2 once gave 100% regen while casting for 30 sec; now 50% for 15 sec ([wfts beta](https://wowforevertalents.com/beta/)).
- 🟡 A forum post notes that the reset "will not trigger on bosses", since players can't usefully be dodged or parried by them (design concern, not a test) ([thread 2365734](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2365734)).
- 🟡 Icy Veins spends its first extra *Talented* points on 2/2 Improved Stormstrike ([Icy Veins Enhancement](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/enhancement-shaman-melee-dps-pve-guide)). See [Mana Regeneration](../mechanics/Mana-Regeneration.md).

### Maelstrom Weapon

**Row 6, Col 3 · 5 ranks · Requires: — · Status: `NEW`**

- 🟢 **Forever:** "When you deal damage with a melee attack, you have a chance to reduce the cast time and Mana cost of your next **Lightning Bolt** spell by `4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 20%`. Stacks up to 5 times. Lasts 30 sec." ([wft](https://wowforevertalent.com/shaman/reference/), [wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/), in-game tooltip quoted in [thread 2356116](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2356116)). Talent spell 408498, buff 408505; DB2 has 20% max per stack and 5 stacks ([wago Spell](https://wago.tools/db2/Spell/csv?build=1.60.1.70170)).
- **vs Classic:** did not exist (it is a Season of Discovery spell ID reused).
- 🟡 **Per-stack reading:** Warcraft Tavern and testers read 5/5 as 20% **per stack**, so 5 stacks = 100% = an **instant, free Lightning Bolt** ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/), [thread 2356116](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2356116)). The tooltip implies this, but **nobody has confirmed it in game**.
- 🟡 **Lightning Bolt only.** It does not work on Chain Lightning or heals: "CL is going to be full cost always" ([thread 2354160](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2354160); [Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/)).
- 🟡 **Slow on two-handers:** "MW procs with a 2 hander are insanely slow"; players estimate 17–20 s for 5 stacks on a 3.5–3.8 speed weapon ([thread 2365290](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/please-fix-shamans/2365290), [2356116](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2356116)). The test conditions were not stated, and at the time the beta cap put this talent out of reach.
- 🟢/🔴 **Proc model unknown:** DB2 SpellAuraOptions for 408498 shows ProcChance 100 with no PPM (procs-per-minute, a weapon-speed-normalized rate) entry, so the real logic is probably elsewhere ([wago SpellAuraOptions](https://wago.tools/db2/SpellAuraOptions/csv?build=1.60.1.70170)).
- Beta history: pre-Sept 24 versions stacked to 5 × rank (up to **25**) and lasted 30 × rank sec (up to 150 sec). Now 5 stacks and 30 sec ([wfts beta](https://wowforevertalents.com/beta/)). "25 stacks" in older guides is that leak artifact.
- 🟡 Icy Veins says Maelstrom Weapon is not worth taking until you can fit all 5 points ([Icy Veins Enhancement](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/enhancement-shaman-melee-dps-pve-guide)).
- Gear: the *Totem of the Storm* relic lets Lightning Bolt itself trigger Maelstrom Weapon at a 50% reduced chance 🟢 ([WoWHandbook relics](https://wowhandbook.com/gear/relics/)).
- 🔵 Downranked spells have a reduced chance to activate talents since Oct 1 ([dev notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696)). Analysis: this may matter if you spend stacks on low-rank Lightning Bolts.
- See [Enhancement Mechanics](../mechanics/Enhancement-Mechanics.md#maelstrom-weapon).

### Rage of the Farseer

**Row 7, Col 2 · 1 rank · Requires: Mental Quickness 2/2 · Status: `NEW` (31-point active)**

- 🟢 **Forever:** active. **Instant, 3 min cooldown.** Attack speed **+30% for 25 sec** ([wfts](https://wowforevertalents.com/shaman/)). Spell ID 425336.
- **Mana cost:** not stated in any source we have (see To Verify). **Trainer ranks:** none; 1 rank only.
- **vs Classic:** did not exist.
- 🔵 **Sept 24:** it no longer increases **spell casting speed**. The tooltip went from "melee attack speed and spell casting speed" to "attack speed", and its second effect was removed from DB2 ([dev notes](https://us.forums.blizzard.com/en/wow/t/2360696), [wago diff in research](https://wago.tools/db2/SpellEffect/csv?build=1.60.1.70170)). 🔴 Conquest Capped still lists "+30% melee/spell speed" (stale) ([Conquest Capped](https://conquestcapped.com/guides/wow-forever/wow-forever-shaman-talents/)).
- 🟡 Guides describe it as a "personal Bloodlust" on a 3 min cooldown ([Warcraft Tavern](https://www.warcrafttavern.com/forever/guides/shaman/), [Icy Veins overview](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow-forever/shaman-class-overview)).
- See [Enhancement Mechanics](../mechanics/Enhancement-Mechanics.md#rage-of-the-farseer).
